On-Site Survey Advice

Insulation Provider: Probe wall thickness at window reveals. Identify solid vs cavity vs timber lath. Check if floorboards can be lifted for underfloor insulation.

Builder / Plumber: Measure existing radiator dimensions and verify pipe diameter (10mm microbore requires higher pump head; 15mm/22mm is ideal for heat pumps).

Cold Roof Buffer

The 2nd floor is not lived in. The insulation layer at the attic floor (ceiling of all 13 First Floor rooms) is the primary thermal envelope boundary.

Insulation Provider Action Item: Top up any first-floor rooms with less than 270mm insulation. Ensure eaves vents are kept clear to prevent timber rot in old roof structure.
